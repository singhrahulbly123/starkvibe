# How to Choose a 350 GSM Fleece Hoodie

Meta title: How to Choose a 350 GSM Fleece Hoodie | StarkVibe

Meta description: Learn about 350 GSM cotton fleece hoodies, regular fit, size charts and custom printing before choosing your colour.

A 350 GSM fleece hoodie is a substantial cotton layer for cooler days. Start with the garment details, compare the available colours, and check the measurements on the product page before choosing a size.

## What 350 GSM means

GSM means grams per square metre. It describes the weight of the fabric. StarkVibe's 350 GSM fleece is the heaviest fabric weight in the range, giving the hoodie a substantial feel for cooler days, travel and everyday wear.

The hoodie is made from 100% cotton fleece. When comparing it with a lighter garment, the GSM number gives you a useful starting point for understanding fabric weight. It does not replace the other details on the product page: the shape, sleeves, hood, pocket and measurements all matter when you choose a piece to wear.

The fabric is double bio-washed and silicon washed, and the seams are double stitched. The finish and construction are part of the product description, alongside the 350 GSM fabric weight. Review those details together so that the garment you select suits the way you plan to use it.

## Look at the hoodie details

The hoodie has a regular fit, a hood, full sleeves and a front kangaroo pocket. These details give it a straightforward shape for everyday layering. The pocket is on the front, while the hood provides an alternative to the open neckline of a crewneck sweatshirt.

Think about where you expect to wear the hoodie. For an everyday layer, you might start by looking at the colour and the fit. For a group, college or team order, it is useful to consider the colour and size breakdown for everyone before sending an enquiry.

Browse the [fleece hoodies](/shop/hoodies) to compare the available colour pages. Each product page brings together the relevant product information and size chart. Check the specific page you intend to order from rather than assuming that every garment has identical options.

## Choose a colour

The hoodie range includes Black, Coffee Brown, Off-white, Olive Green and Dark Beige. Each colour has its own product page. Choose the colour that works for your wardrobe or, for a group order, the colour that suits the shared design.

When planning a custom design, include your preferred garment colour in the enquiry. Share the design and the quantity as well as the sizes you need. Having those details together makes it easier for the team to prepare a quote for the garment you have chosen.

If you are ordering for several people, make a list of the required sizes and quantities. Check the current product pages for the options available when you are ready to order. Use the same list when discussing your design so that the garment and the print request stay clear.

## Check the size chart

Start with your usual size and check the [size chart](/size-guide). Product pages list chest, shoulder, length and sleeve measurements in inches. Compare these measurements with your requirements before selecting a size.

The hoodie has a regular fit. You can begin with your normal size for an everyday fit; if you prefer a roomier look, consider going one size up. The size label is a starting point, while the measurements give you more information about the garment itself.

For a group order, ask each person to check the measurements and share the size they need. Keep the list with your colour and quantity information. This is especially useful when the same design will be used across several sizes.

If you are deciding between a hoodie and a sweatshirt, compare both styles before choosing. The [crewneck sweatshirts](/shop/sweatshirts) are also made from 350 GSM cotton fleece. They have a crewneck pullover shape and long sleeves, while the hoodie has a hood and front kangaroo pocket.

## Plan a custom print

The hoodie can be used as a base for custom printing for colleges, teams, events or campaigns. Start with the garment colour and make a clear list of the quantities and sizes. Then share the design along with those details through the [custom printing page](/custom-printing).

If you are planning for a group, one person can collect the size choices before sending the enquiry. Include the total quantity and the breakdown by size. That keeps the request clear and allows the team to respond with a quote based on the information provided.

Minimum quantities and turnaround are confirmed in the quote. If a detail of the design or order is still undecided, mention that when you enquire. The product page remains the place to review the current garment information, while the custom-printing enquiry is where you can discuss your design and order requirements.

## Care and common questions

Follow the garment care label. Wash the hoodie inside out with similar colours in cold or normal water and use mild detergent. Do not use bleach or scrub harshly. Dry in shade and, if needed, iron at a low temperature.

### Is 350 GSM a fabric weight?

Yes. GSM stands for grams per square metre. The number describes fabric weight; 350 GSM is the heaviest weight in the StarkVibe range.

### How should I choose between sizes?

Start with your usual size, then check the chest, shoulder, length and sleeve measurements. Consider a size up if you prefer a roomier look.

### Can I request my own design?

Yes. Send your design, quantity and sizes through the custom-printing enquiry. Minimum quantities and turnaround are confirmed in your quote.
